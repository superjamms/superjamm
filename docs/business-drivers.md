# Business Drivers

## Financial

- Increase revenue
  - Attract venue customers (for restaurants/bars, more service revenue)
  - Cover fees (for higher tier performers/open jamm features)
  - Higher tier / open jams warrant higher prices for house band and open jam coordination

- Evangelize house band, sponsors, venue
  - this may be hard to quantify in direct revenue terms,
    but indirectly measurable by relationships/referrals

## Efficiency

- Offload part of open jam event coordination to an app
    - Matching sit-ins, house-band, or backing track requirements
    - Song/Set lists and scheduling to fit in an open-jam event

- Identify ways to reduce workload for roles:
  - Roadie (setup/teardown)
  - Sound engineer
  - House band
  - Sit-ins (performers not in the house band)
